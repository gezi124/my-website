Company Profile

让农田作业走向无人化Towards Unmanned Field Operations

宁波轮上机器人有限公司（WHEELBOTS）是智能化模块化农业机器人领军企业。我们移植汽车行业的电动化与智能化技术，深耕农田自动驾驶及机器视觉，以通用型农业机器人平台实现农田无人化作业，赋能世界。

Wheelbots Co. Ltd. (WHEELBOTS) is a leader in intelligent, modular agricultural robots. We apply automotive electrification and intelligence to field autonomy and machine vision, enabling unmanned operations on universal robot platforms.

以客户为中心，扁平化组织与敏捷响应机制，配合高质量供应链与一流运营服务，坚持优质平价的产品定位。

A customer-centric approach, flat organization and agile response, backed by a quality supply chain and top-tier operations — quality at an affordable price.
